How to read the map. Each country reports a different index (Singapore PSI, Malaysia API, Indonesia ISPU, and US AQI from AQI.in), so the numbers are not comparable across borders. Colours use each index's own category bands. Indonesian ISPU values above the usual 500 ceiling are shown as the environment ministry reported them.

Smoke drift bands are drawn from the wind directions and source regions named by NEA, not from satellite plume outlines. Hotspot diamonds use the Forestry Ministry's high-confidence count for 7 October, 9 pm WIB; provincial agency counts that include lower-confidence detections are much higher.

When to get medical help

Irritated eyes, nose or throat are common in haze and usually clear on their own. Symptoms can show up one to three days after exposure.

See a doctor if you feel unwell, and do it promptly for elderly people, children, pregnant women and anyone with a heart or lung condition. If someone is struggling to breathe or has chest pain, call 995.
